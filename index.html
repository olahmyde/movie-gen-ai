    Four steps from an idea to a film you can download.

    1. Start the GPU

      Switch it on in the top bar. It bills by the hour until you stop it.

    2. Describe the film

      Write the idea on the left. The script comes back as scenes, with lines for each character.

    3. Make voices and clips

      Pick a voice for each character, then make the voices and a clip for every scene.

    4. Build the movie

      The clips are stitched into one MP4 you can watch here and download.
